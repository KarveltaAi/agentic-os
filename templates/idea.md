# Idea title template

> Copy into `agentic-os-vault/05-Ideas/<slug>.md` and fill in every section.
> `status` in the frontmatter drives the `/ideas` intake pipeline — see
> CLAUDE.md "Idea-to-release pipeline". Valid values: `new`, `in-board`,
> `in-discovery`, `approved`, `parked`, `rejected`.

## One-line pitch

The idea in a single sentence.

## Problem

What problem does this solve, and for whom? What's broken or missing today?

## Why now

Timing, trigger, or evidence that made this idea worth writing down.

## Rough shape

Sketch of what it might look like — not a spec, just enough for the board
to react to.

## Who it's for

Target user/customer, and (if known) the project or client it relates to.
"New/own product" if not tied to an existing project.

## Open questions / risks

What's unknown or risky enough that the board should probe it.
